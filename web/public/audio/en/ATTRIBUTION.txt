English course audio (Comic City Quest)

Original lesson texts by the Gray Crown project. Audio is synthesized speech, not a recording
of a live teacher. Generated with Piper using the en_GB-cori-medium voice (22,050 Hz),
length_scale 1.12, then encoded to mono 48 kbps MP3 with ffmpeg (libmp3lame).

Voice/model: https://huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices/tree/main/en/en_GB/cori/medium
Model author: Bryce Beattie (https://brycebeattie.com/files/tts/)
Training data: LibriVox recordings (public domain), per the model card.
Piper synthesis tool (build-time only): https://github.com/rhasspy/piper
Reproduction: scripts/generate-english-audio.mjs
The Piper executable and model are not required by learners and are not bundled.
