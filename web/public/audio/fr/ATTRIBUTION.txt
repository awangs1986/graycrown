French A1 course audio

Original lesson texts by the Gray Crown project. Audio is synthesized speech,
not a recording of a live teacher. Generated with Piper using the
fr_FR-siwis-medium voice (22,050 Hz), length_scale 1.15.

Voice/model: https://huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices/tree/main/fr/fr_FR/siwis/medium
Dataset: SIWIS French Speech Synthesis Database
Authors: Pierre-Edouard Honnet, Alexandros Lazaridis, Philip N. Garner, Junichi Yamagishi
Source: https://datashare.ed.ac.uk/handle/10283/2353
License indicated by model card: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
Changes: synthesized new utterances from original A1 lesson text, slowed to 1.15 length scale.

Piper synthesis tool (build-time only): https://github.com/rhasspy/piper
Reproduction: scripts/generate-french-audio.mjs
The Piper executable and model are not required by learners and are not bundled.
