# English · Comic City Quest — credits and content policy

## Syllabus alignment (原创内容声明)
The unit order follows the grammar and topic sequence of *New Concept English* Books 1–2
(《新概念英语》第一、二册，Longman / 外语教学与研究出版社). **Aligned to the NCE syllabus, original content**:
every lesson title, dialogue, story, sentence, vocabulary selection and exercise in this folder was written
for Gray Crown. No NCE lesson text, dialogue, title, illustration or exercise is reproduced. "New Concept English"
is used only to describe the syllabus the course follows; the course is not affiliated with or endorsed by its
authors or publishers.

本课程仅对齐新概念英语第一、二册的语法与话题顺序，所有课文、对话、标题与练习均为原创。

## Artwork
All illustrations are composed at runtime from the hand-authored SVG kit in `art.mjs` / `shared/comic-kit.mjs` (places, the recurring
original cast Leo, Mia, Sam, Mr Grant, Grandma Rose, Dr Lin, Ada, Officer Bell, and the trickster Mr Muddle,
plus everyday props). No raster images, no third-party or franchise characters.
