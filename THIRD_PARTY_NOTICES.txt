French A1 audio:
Synthesized with Piper using fr_FR-siwis-medium. SIWIS dataset/model card specifies
CC-BY 4.0. See web/public/audio/fr/ATTRIBUTION.txt (distributed as audio/fr/ATTRIBUTION.txt)
for authors, source, license and modifications. Lesson text is original project content.

English course audio:
Synthesized with Piper using en_GB-cori-medium (model card: trained on public-domain LibriVox
recordings), encoded to MP3 with ffmpeg/libmp3lame. See web/public/audio/en/ATTRIBUTION.txt.
Lesson text is original project content aligned to the New Concept English Books 1-2 syllabus;
no New Concept English text is reproduced. Comic illustrations (English and French) are original SVG from the project's shared comic kit
(web/courses/shared/comic-kit.mjs); no third-party artwork is included.
